How Domain Purchase Works

Every step is handled by our team. You're never charged without an approved quote.

  1. Submit a request

    Tell us the domain you want or the one you already own.

  2. Manual availability check

    Our team checks availability and the actual registration price with the registrar.

  3. You receive a quote

    The quote shows the registration price, service fee (if any), registration term, and renewal price.

  4. You review and approve

    Nothing proceeds until you explicitly approve the quote.

  5. Payment

    You pay through a payment method our team has confirmed. Payment status and registration status are tracked separately.

  6. Registration, DNS & SSL

    After payment is verified, the domain is registered and DNS and HTTPS are configured.

  7. Connected

    The domain is marked connected only after DNS propagation and HTTPS access are both verified.

    Need Help?

    Common questions about domains, DNS, and renewals.

    Choosing a domain name

    Short, memorable, and easy to spell beats clever or punny. Aim for fewer than 16 characters.

    Understanding pricing

    Registration and renewal prices are confirmed in a quote from our team before you pay.

    Connecting an existing domain

    If you already own a domain, we'll guide you through pointing its DNS records to your portfolio.

    DNS configuration

    DNS changes can take a few hours to propagate. We'll confirm once records are verified.

    HTTPS & SSL

    A certificate is issued and verified by our team after DNS is confirmed. HTTPS is only marked active once verified.

    Purchase & payment status

    Payment status and registration status are tracked separately in your requests list.
