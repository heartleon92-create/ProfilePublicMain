About

I'm a full stack developer based in Yangon, working across the stack to ship product interfaces that feel considered and load fast. Over the last two years I've helped teams turn rough design files into accessible, maintainable front-end systems.

My day-to-day is TypeScript, React and Node, paired with a strong bias toward semantic HTML and measurable performance budgets. I care about the details that rarely make it into a screenshot: keyboard paths, loading and empty states, and error copy that actually helps.

2 years shipping design systems and product UI. Comfortable owning a feature end to end — from the API contract through to the last focus ring.
