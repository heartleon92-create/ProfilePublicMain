Common Questions

Answers to what customers usually ask.

What is the difference between a Business Website and a POS?

A Business Website is customer-facing — it helps customers see and understand your business online. A POS / Business Management System is internal — it is used by owners, managers and staff to manage sales, orders, products, inventory and reports.

Can I start with a low-cost deployment?

Yes. Static or customer-facing websites may use free hosting options such as GitHub Pages when appropriate. This is a good fit for profiles and simple business websites.

Do I need a .com domain?

Not necessarily. It depends on the project and the customer's requirements. A .com domain is recommended for professional business presences, but it is optional for budget deployments.

Can Profile Craft build a custom system?

Yes, if the required functions and technical scope are suitable for the project. We will discuss the modules, data and infrastructure needed before confirming the build.

Can a Profile Website have functions?

Yes. Depending on requirements, a profile can include interactive or data-driven functions such as appointment booking, schedules or service listings. Advanced features are project-dependent — they are not included by default.
