Stage 01

Requirement Discussion

Before proposing any technical solution, we take time to understand your business, profession, or use case in detail. We listen closely to your core objectives.

What We Discuss:

  • Your business, profession, or specific use case.
  • Main problem the website or system should solve.
  • Target users and expected functionality.
  • Required pages, modules or interactive features.
  • Preferred visual direction and branding.
  • Budget expectations and desired timeline.
  • Existing content, images, data or systems, if applicable.
Customer Contribution: Explain needs and provide available info, examples or reference websites.
Expected Outcome: A clearer understanding of project requirements and next steps.

Stage 02

Planning & Scope

We convert initial requirements into a defined project scope, outlining exact deliverables, technical approach, cost estimation, and schedule before any development begins.

What Planning Covers:

  • Page structure for personal or business websites.
  • Modules and screens for POS / management systems.
  • Required interactive functions and technical approach.
  • Estimated cost, development schedule, inclusions and exclusions.
Important Rule: Prices and timelines depend on complexity and confirmed scope. Additional features may affect cost and delivery time. We never promise fixed completion dates before requirements are understood.
Stage 03

UI/UX Design

We plan how your website or system looks and how users interact with it. Our design process ensures clean hierarchy, responsive layouts, and intuitive navigation.

Design Scope Includes:

  • Page layout and content hierarchy.
  • Navigation and user flows.
  • Typography, colors, and spacing.
  • Responsive desktop and mobile layouts.
  • Dashboard or system screen designs for POS projects.
Customer Review & Approval: Review proposed design direction and provide consolidated feedback before implementation begins.

Stage 04

Development

We turn the approved scope and design into a fully functioning digital product. Implementation details vary based on the specific service package selected.

Implementation by Service Type:

• Personal Profile: Personal content, professional presentation, custom interactive JavaScript functions, booking/scheduling calculators.
• Business Website: Business info, service presentation, product/menu displays, gallery, contact links, and responsive layouts.
• POS / Business Management System: Agreed application screens, backend logic, database integration, sales/inventory reports, and access permissions.
Customer Contribution: Provide agreed text, images, business info, product data, and respond promptly to clarification requests.
Stage 05

Testing & Review

We verify that the implementation works according to agreed requirements. We conduct rigorous testing across common browsers, screen sizes, and workflows.

What We Test & Verify:

  • Core functionality and interactive workflows.
  • Links, navigation, forms, and data validation.
  • Responsive behavior on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
  • Database operations and browser compatibility.
Important Rules for Testing: Testing ensures agreed features function correctly, but does not guarantee an entirely error-free ecosystem under rare third-party conditions. Issues in scope are fixed; newly requested features require separate scope/quotation.

Stage 06

Deployment & Handover

We publish your finished project live and provide a structured handover so you or your team can manage the platform with confidence.

Deployment & Handover Details:

  • Static hosting via Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages where applicable.
  • Custom domain and paid hosting configuration when planned.
  • Secure handover of credentials, source repository access, and basic user walkthrough.
  • Clear separation between development fees and recurring domain/hosting costs.
Important Note on Infrastructure: Domain registration, hosting, ongoing maintenance, and training are not automatically included unless explicitly specified in your project agreement. We do not operate our own hosting infrastructure; third-party fees apply separately.

How We Keep Projects Clear

Our working principles are designed to eliminate confusion and maintain complete transparency throughout the project.

Clear Requirements First

We establish exact needs and objectives before writing a single line of code, ensuring total alignment from day one.

Confirmed Scope & Deliverables

Project scope, timeline, and deliverables are fully documented and agreed upon prior to implementation.

Transparent Requirements & Cost

Any additional requirements or scope modifications are communicated clearly with separate estimations.

Testing Against Agreed Functionality

Every feature is rigorously checked against the approved specifications before final review and handover.

Structured Approval Checkpoints

Customer review and sign-off are required at critical milestones such as design and pre-deployment testing.

Separation of Development & Infrastructure

Development service fees are clearly distinguished from recurring third-party domain and hosting costs.
